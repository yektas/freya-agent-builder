- Supporting evidence: all eight account-bearing historical calls exhibit that boundary failure; the new static checker rejects that consumer pattern. Synthetic producer/consumer tests parse complete nested snapshots under a string-variable boundary.
- Regressions/missing cases: live platform snapshot persistence, identity behavior, generation acceptance rate, token/latency growth and end-to-end policy coverage remain unmeasured. Dynamic response accesses and incorrect state predicates can evade static checks.
- Uncertainty/noise: historical 1/10 is one repetition and cannot be compared causally with synthetic checks or new unmatched runs. Rejecting a bad candidate does not establish that a better candidate will be generated.
- Decision: the user selected runtime-contract as the default for new CLI and inspector generations. Direct remains explicitly selectable for comparisons. This is a user-selected default, not evidence of behavioral superiority. Historical records without a strategy remain classified as direct. No practice results are changed.
