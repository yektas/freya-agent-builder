## Earliest consequential failure in the trace

V15 routed from `contact_and_identity` to `closing` immediately after the named customer confirmed who answered. It asked no security question and called no `verify_identity` tool. `closing` then invented a service outage. See `docs/plans/2026-10-11-v15-premature-closing.md` for call and ledger evidence.

## Design and fallback

The variant keeps the same original briefs, OpenAPI, platform documents, model, and generation budget as direct. Its appended instructions ask the model to separate contact, verification, and closure, route on authoritative tool results, and use one concrete customer signal per intent condition. The first candidate rejected descriptions over 160 characters before upload. The revision removes that ceiling and all fixed graph-size caps. It reports node, transition, and maximum per-node intent counts as diagnostics, while still rejecting a missing intent description. No tool schemas or task facts are added to generated output. Direct generation remains available with unchanged instructions.

Counts help identify a graph worth reviewing; they cannot establish a safe threshold. The seven-node v15 also failed, while the 59-node and 38-node candidates failed in different ways. The kit says `routing_prompt` replaces the default intent-matching instruction. The second candidate's account node used a custom prompt, and its router marked every exit false even after explicit paid and lower-instalment statements. This is a plausible mechanism, not proof that all custom routing prompts fail. The third revision asks the builder to omit them on semantic forks unless the rule cannot be expressed in the transitions, and records their count. New behavior requires a fresh generated workflow and live practice evidence before promotion.

The first candidate compiled in 365.8 seconds with one model call, 33,102 input and 15,026 output tokens, estimated at $0.216464. It contains 59 nodes, 142 transitions, up to 13 intent routes per node, and no extraction fields. Its longest `matches_intent` description is 95 characters. The contact-confirmed route goes to a code marker and then the security node; the first security tool is `verify_identity`. Upload registered workflow `04e33a46-9942-428b-af78-adf4d44d1da5` and agent `511a2d21-600a-482c-a8b3-7fb616beee8e` once. Practice job `da3f8f80-68f0-4bb2-a9d5-1a5eb05d3929` finished with a valid 0/10. Registration remained separate from generation and no repair call occurred.

## Regressions and decision

- PUB-01, PUB-04, PUB-07, and PUB-10 reached account work but did not complete the required promise and close-call path. PUB-07 and PUB-10 looped after attempting to finish.
- PUB-02 treated “I’m her husband” as a wrong number instead of arranging the required callback. PUB-03 made four incorrect verification attempts and failed to close. PUB-05 repeated sympathy and tried to persuade after hardship. PUB-06 treated inability to pay the full amount as hardship rather than negotiating the minimum. PUB-08 transferred an already-paid case to support. PUB-09 transferred restructuring to the wrong queue.
- These are observed regressions of one candidate and one practice repetition. They do not prove that every large graph fails or every compact graph succeeds. The earlier direct 6/10 build was smaller, but direct scores have varied, and v15 failed with only seven nodes.
- The revised candidate's first generation was accepted but failed with provider HTTP 500 after a partial draft. It is an unmeasured failed build, not a routing regression. A single retry with the same instructions hash was started separately.
- The retry compiled in 277 s with one model call and produced 38 nodes, 98 transitions, and up to 11 intent routes in one node. It uploaded once as workflow `07ae1432-0885-4610-b166-67ff21ff2a58` / agent `9d6cba73-8f0f-4ae2-b1c7-b9b0582f2e59`. Its one Brief A practice repetition scored **2/10 valid**.
- PUB-01 and PUB-04 passed. PUB-02 routed a husband to wrong number rather than scheduling a callback. PUB-03 failed to direct the customer to the app or branch and did not close properly. PUB-05 ignored hardship and continued persuasion. PUB-06 offered and recorded 50 rather than the source-derived 637.50 minimum. PUB-07 recorded October 23 rather than the next business day, October 27. PUB-08 missed the already-paid route until after over 140 goodbye turns. PUB-09 missed repeated restructuring requests and never transferred. PUB-10 used “recorded” in the opening before the promise succeeded.
- The PUB-08 and PUB-09 call traces show the account router evaluating all 11 intent exits false after explicit customer signals, then taking the always self-route. The account node's custom `routing_prompt` replaces the platform's default intent instruction, which may explain this behavior. The trace establishes missed routes and loops; it does not isolate the custom prompt as the sole cause.
- The third candidate compiled with zero custom routing prompts. It has 49 nodes and 149 transitions, including 19 intent routes at the busiest node; these counts are diagnostics, not a hard rejection. It uploaded once as workflow `5810a417-91d8-448b-ae17-9dc36965cb62` / agent `b0dc361c-dd72-4ba4-a717-f76bd6bd1087`. One Brief A practice run scored **6/10 valid**, matching the best recorded direct result in one repetition but without a matched build or reliability repetitions.
- PUB-01, PUB-04, PUB-06, PUB-07, PUB-08, and PUB-09 passed both the ledger and conversation checks. PUB-02 routed to the third-party stage, but after confirmation its dialogue printed pseudo callback and close-call commands instead of reaching the actual `schedule_callback` and `close_call` nodes. It repeated goodbye until the customer ended the call; the judge approved the apparent booking but the ledger had neither write. PUB-03 obtained a `wrong_answers_left: 0` result, then asked the security question once more before routing to verification failure. PUB-05 responded to a hardship disclosure in the prior security stage, then repeated sympathy in the hardship stage and made a payment attempt. PUB-10 recorded exactly one promise after a retry, but its judge treated the brief-required opening disclosure “this call is recorded” as a premature promise-recorded claim. This is a conflict in the practice assessment, not evidence that the ledger write occurred early.
- The fourth prompt revision asks for a separate `api_request` stage when the next dialogue must wait for an authoritative tool result, an explicit confirmation route to delayed writes, and state for one-time language. These are general routing mechanics; their effect is unmeasured.
- Its first generation was accepted but ended with provider HTTP 500 after a partial draft. It is an unmeasured infrastructure failure. One bounded retry with the same instructions is in progress.
- Decision: **reject the first two candidates; keep the third experimental**. Its one 6/10 run is a tie with direct, not evidence for promotion. Keep direct as default. The fourth revision must be tested before any performance claim.
